Privacy

CareerCrumbs · last updated November 3, 2026

The short version: everything you write in CareerCrumbs stays on your device unless you choose to sign in. Accounts are optional. There are no analytics, no ads and no AI.

What CareerCrumbs stores

Your jobs, interview answers, stories, practice history and settings are saved in your browser's storage on your device (or inside the CareerCrumbs app's storage on Android). We never receive a copy.

If you sign in (optional)

Signing in lets you back up your work and use it on more than one device. When you sign in, we store:

  • Your email address, to send you sign-in codes and identify your account.
  • A copy of your CareerCrumbs data (jobs, interview answers, stories, practice history and settings), so it can sync.

This is stored with our database provider, Supabase, and sent over an encrypted connection. Each account can only read its own data. We don't sell it, share it or use it for anything except syncing it back to you.

You can sign out any time; your data stays on your device. Me → Delete my account deletes your account and the copy stored with it right away. If you can't get into the app, email us (below) from the address you signed in with and we'll delete it for you.

PIN lock (optional)

If you set a PIN, your data on the device is encrypted with a key made from your PIN. The PIN itself is never stored or sent anywhere, so we can't recover it. If you forget it, you can erase the app's data on that device and sign in again (or import a backup) to get your work back.

What leaves your device

  • If you're signed in, your synced data (see above).
  • Otherwise nothing, unless you choose to. A backup file, a calendar file or a copied message only goes where you send it: your downloads, your calendar app, an email or a text.
  • Job posting links you add open in your browser like any other link.
  • The app, its fonts and its icons are downloaded from the CareerCrumbs website when you first open it, as with any website. We don't log or keep any information about those requests ourselves; the hosting provider may keep standard server logs.

What we don't do

  • No sign-up required; accounts are optional.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, advertising or selling of data.
  • No AI: your answers are never sent to an AI service.

Deleting your data

On your device: Me → Erase everything, clear the site's data in your browser, or uninstall the app. In your account: Me → Delete my account, or email us. Deleted data can't be recovered unless you exported a backup.

Children

CareerCrumbs is meant for people looking for work and isn't directed at children under 13.
