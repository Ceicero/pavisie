Privacy

CareerCrumbs · last updated November 2, 2026

The short version: everything you write in CareerCrumbs stays on your device. There is no account, no server that stores your data, no analytics, no ads and no AI.

What CareerCrumbs stores

Your jobs, interview answers, stories, practice history and settings are saved in your browser's storage on your device (or inside the CareerCrumbs app's storage on Android). We never receive a copy.

What leaves your device

  • Nothing, unless you choose to. A backup file, a calendar file or a copied message only goes where you send it: your downloads, your calendar app, an email or a text.
  • Job posting links you add open in your browser like any other link.
  • The app, its fonts and its icons are downloaded from the CareerCrumbs website when you first open it, as with any website. We don't log or keep any information about those requests ourselves; the hosting provider may keep standard server logs.

What we don't do

  • No sign-up or account.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, advertising or selling of data.
  • No AI: your answers are never sent to an AI service.

Deleting your data

Open Me → Erase everything, clear the site's data in your browser, or uninstall the app. Because there's no server copy, deleted data can't be recovered unless you exported a backup.

Children

CareerCrumbs is meant for people looking for work and isn't directed at children under 13.
